SyncMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

This policy explains how SyncMind ("the app", "we") handles your information. The app is operated by BitbytesTech. Contact: support.bitbytestech@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile identifier. Guests can play without signing in and get an anonymous player id.
  • Game data: your username, progress, scores, diamonds, power-ups and game history, stored on our server so it syncs across sessions.
  • Purchases: when you buy diamonds or power-ups, Google Play processes the payment. We receive a purchase token and product id to verify it and credit your account. We never see your card details.
  • Device and usage data: a push notification token, app version, device type and basic analytics and crash information.
  • Advertising data: if you watch rewarded ads, Google AdMob may collect your advertising id and device information, as described in Google's privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To provide the game, keep your progress and credit purchases and rewards.
  • To send notifications you allow (you can turn them off in your device settings).
  • To show rewarded ads, prevent fraud and improve the app.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers needed to run the app: Google (sign-in, Firebase push notifications and analytics, Google Play billing, AdMob) and our hosting provider.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. To delete your account and data, email support.bitbytestech@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
